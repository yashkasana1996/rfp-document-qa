REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL — INTERIOR FIT-OUT WORKS
Northwind Analytics Pvt Ltd (fictional) — New Regional Office, Tower B, Levels 7 & 8
RFP Reference: NWA/FO/2026/014 — Issue date: 2 November 2026
THIS IS A SYNTHETIC SAMPLE DOCUMENT CREATED FOR DEMONSTRATION. ALL NAMES, FIGURES AND TERMS ARE INVENTED.

SECTION 1 — INTRODUCTION
1.1 Northwind Analytics Pvt Ltd ("the Client") invites sealed proposals from qualified design-and-build contractors for the complete interior fit-out of approximately 48,000 sq ft of carpet area across Levels 7 and 8 of Tower B.
1.2 The scope covers civil and interior works, mechanical, electrical and plumbing (MEP) services, HVAC, fire detection and suppression, IT/AV containment, loose furniture and signage.
1.3 The Client's project management consultant (PMC) for this engagement is Tailspin Project Advisory (fictional). All queries must be routed through the PMC.

SECTION 2 — KEY DATES
2.1 Pre-bid site visit: 9 November 2026 at 11:00 hrs. Attendance is mandatory for at least one representative of each bidder.
2.2 Last date for written queries: 12 November 2026, 17:00 hrs.
2.3 Proposal submission deadline: 23 November 2026, 15:00 hrs. Late submissions will not be opened.
2.4 Technical bid opening: 24 November 2026. Commercial bids of technically qualified bidders will be opened on 1 December 2026.
2.5 Target date for issue of Letter of Award: 10 December 2026.

SECTION 3 — ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
3.1 Average annual turnover of at least INR 60 crore over the last three audited financial years (FY2023-24, FY2024-25, FY2025-26).
3.2 Completion of at least three similar interior fit-out projects of a minimum 30,000 sq ft each in the last five years, or two projects of 45,000 sq ft each. Completion certificates from clients are mandatory.
3.3 Valid GST registration and PAN, and ISO 9001 and ISO 45001 certification.
3.4 The bidder must not be blacklisted by any government body or listed company. A self-declaration on letterhead is required.
3.5 Consortium bids and joint ventures are not permitted. Subcontracting of MEP packages is permitted with prior written approval of the PMC.

SECTION 4 — PROJECT TIMELINE
4.1 The total completion period is 18 weeks from the date of handover of site, including design development.
4.2 Design development and approval of good-for-construction drawings must be completed within 4 weeks of site handover.
4.3 Intermediate milestone: completion of all above-ceiling MEP services by the end of Week 11.
4.4 The contractor shall submit a detailed programme (Primavera or MS Project) within 7 days of Letter of Award.

SECTION 5 — COMMERCIAL TERMS
5.1 Contract type: lump-sum, fixed price, with re-measurable provisional sums only for items marked "PS" in the BOQ.
5.2 Prices shall be quoted exclusive of GST. GST will be paid at the applicable rate against valid tax invoices.
5.3 Payment terms:
    (a) Mobilisation advance of 10% of contract value against an unconditional bank guarantee of equal value, recovered pro-rata from running bills.
    (b) Running account bills monthly, payable within 30 days of certification by the PMC, at 85% of certified value.
    (c) 10% on virtual completion and handover.
    (d) Balance 5% retention released on expiry of the defects liability period, or earlier against a bank guarantee of equal value.
5.4 Price variation: none. Prices are firm until completion, including any extension of time attributable to the contractor.

SECTION 6 — EARNEST MONEY DEPOSIT AND SECURITIES
6.1 Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) of INR 25,00,000 by demand draft or bank guarantee valid for 120 days from the bid due date. Bids without EMD will be rejected.
6.2 Performance bank guarantee of 5% of contract value, valid until 60 days beyond the defects liability period, within 14 days of Letter of Award.

SECTION 7 — LIQUIDATED DAMAGES AND PENALTIES
7.1 Delay in overall completion: liquidated damages at 0.5% of contract value per week of delay, capped at 5% of contract value.
7.2 Failure to achieve the Week 11 MEP milestone: withholding of 2% of the next running bill, released if overall completion is achieved on time.
7.3 If delay exceeds 6 weeks beyond the completion date, the Client may terminate for default, encash the performance guarantee and complete the works at the contractor's risk and cost.
7.4 Safety non-compliance observed by the PMC: INR 25,000 per instance, deducted from the running bill.

SECTION 8 — WARRANTY, DEFECTS AND INSURANCE
8.1 Defects liability period: 12 months from the date of virtual completion.
8.2 Manufacturer warranties for HVAC equipment (minimum 3 years) and waterproofing (minimum 5 years) shall be assigned to the Client.
8.3 The contractor shall maintain a Contractor's All Risk policy for 110% of contract value, workmen's compensation, and third-party liability insurance of not less than INR 5 crore.

SECTION 9 — TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS (SUMMARY)
9.1 Materials shall conform to the approved make list in Annexure C. Any deviation requires written approval and a cost-neutral or cost-saving proposal.
9.2 The project targets IGBC Green Interiors Gold certification; the contractor shall maintain documentation for all credits within its scope.
9.3 Work at height above 3 m requires permits and certified scaffolding; hot work requires a permit-to-work.
9.4 Working hours: 08:00–20:00 on weekdays. Noisy works (core cutting, chasing) only between 20:00 and 06:00 as building rules require.

SECTION 10 — SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS
10.1 The technical envelope shall contain:
    (a) Covering letter and signed RFP acceptance on letterhead
    (b) Company profile and organisation chart of the proposed site team
    (c) Audited financial statements for three years and a CA-certified turnover statement
    (d) Completion certificates for qualifying projects (Section 3.2)
    (e) GST, PAN, ISO certificates and the non-blacklisting declaration
    (f) Preliminary design concept and material board (maximum 20 pages)
    (g) Draft programme and method statement for MEP works
    (h) EMD instrument (Section 6.1)
10.2 The commercial envelope shall contain only the priced BOQ in the Client's format, signed and stamped on every page, and a soft copy in Excel. Any alteration to the BOQ format or descriptions will lead to rejection.
10.3 Proposals remain valid for 120 days from the submission deadline.

SECTION 11 — EVALUATION
11.1 Technical evaluation carries 40% weight: relevant experience (15), design concept (10), team (8), programme and methodology (7). A minimum technical score of 28 out of 40 is required to qualify.
11.2 Commercial evaluation carries 60% weight, scored as (lowest qualified price / bidder's price) x 60.
11.3 The Client reserves the right to negotiate with the highest-ranked bidder and to award the works in packages.

SECTION 12 — GENERAL CONDITIONS
12.1 The contract shall be governed by the laws of India, with courts at Mumbai having exclusive jurisdiction. Disputes shall first go to mediation, then arbitration by a sole arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated at Mumbai.
12.2 Confidentiality: all drawings and information shared under this RFP are confidential and must be returned or destroyed if the bidder does not submit a proposal.
12.3 The Client is not bound to accept the lowest or any proposal and may cancel the RFP without assigning reasons.
